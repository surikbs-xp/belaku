Active strategy

Surikbs · GIFT Nifty signal

Every completed 5-minute GIFT candle is checked. All four entry checks must pass. Signals come from GIFT Nifty; orders use the front-month Nifty future.

Entry rules

No EMA 200 filter

BUY

All 4 checks required
  • Trend: at least 2 of 3 SuperTrends bullish
  • RSI(14): below 20
  • TSV(13): above 0
  • PSAR: candle close above Parabolic SAR

SELL

All 4 checks required
  • Trend: at least 2 of 3 SuperTrends bearish
  • RSI(14): above 80
  • TSV(13): below 0
  • PSAR: candle close below Parabolic SAR

SuperTrends use ATR length × multiplier 10×1, 11×2, and 12×3. TSV uses the bot's tick-count volume proxy.

Live rule checks

—

Collecting closed candles before evaluating the rules.

A signal fires on the first closed candle where all four checks agree. The bot does not repeat entries while that condition remains true. Exits are on an opposite signal or manual; there is no stop-loss or profit target. The Pine script's custom SuperTrend/divergence visuals are not entry filters.
